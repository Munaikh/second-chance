# Three-minute demo

Start local services, confirm `/health` and launch the app. Tap the avatar → Reset all demo progress. Keep Noor selected. Use fictional details only. Check audio and ElevenLabs quota before presenting; text remains an equivalent route.

1. **Prepare · 30 seconds.** “Meet Noor. Her income varies, but her bills still have dates.” Show £485 available and £180 short on 12 November. Open **Explore your future**. Rent and energy arrive before earnings on 14 November. These are synthetic records and estimated daily closing balances.
2. **Listen · 30 seconds.** Open **Talk through this scenario** or **Let’s talk**. Ask “Why might I run short?” then “What should I do first?” For voice, tap **Talk with Pip**, allow the microphone and show listening/speaking feedback, mute and stop. Text needs no microphone.
3. **Compare · 30 seconds.** Set the weekly spending reduction to £25. The first gap falls from £180 to £155. Small changes help without erasing the timing problem. Reset for the baseline recovery request.
4. **Support · 45 seconds.** Open **Make a recovery plan**, choose **Less income**, optionally add a fictional note and create the plan. Review the editable request, change a bracketed detail and copy it. Show evidence, source links and the real n8n run ID. Nothing is sent.
5. **Grow · 35 seconds.** At the root, open Grow. Create a £500 buffer and record £5 already set aside. Show the progress milestone and credit-report action. Restart the app and reopen Grow to demonstrate persistence.
6. **Contrast · 10 seconds.** Switch to Alex. Balance, forecast, circumstances and guidance change. Noor’s progress stays with Noor.

## One-minute pitch

Financial difficulty often begins as a timing problem: rent arrives on Thursday, income on Saturday, and someone feels they have already failed. Most dashboards show what happened. Second Chance shows what is approaching and helps someone find a useful step while there is still time.

We connect Prepare, Support and Grow. A transparent cash-flow forecast explains the date and cause of a gap. Pip makes it approachable through voice or text. A personalised recovery workflow turns worry into an editable request, with evidence and sources. Small goals then help someone rebuild a buffer.

The originality is the connection between financial time travel, supportive conversation and practical follow-through. We reward understanding and positive action, not borrowing or spending. Irregular earners and people with limited financial confidence can engage in their own words and at their own pace.

For NatWest, this could offer an earlier, more humane route into existing support, help customers prepare for money worries and support longer-term resilience. These are potential benefits to validate with customers, not measured prototype outcomes.

## Service recovery

- n8n: notes and existing plans stay saved. Restart services and retry; the app does not invent a successful run.
- ElevenLabs: calculated fallback guidance is labelled. Reconnect or use text; fallback is not represented as live AI.
- Microphone: continue through text or enable permission in Settings. Stop voice ends capture and playback while retaining the transcript.
